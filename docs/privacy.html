Privacy Policy

Cartrend Auto Parts Private Limited (“Cartrends”, “we”, “us”) · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cartrend Auto Parts Private Limited collects, uses and protects information when you interact with our business services, including our WhatsApp Business messaging services, order-management systems and website.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information — your name, business/firm name, phone number and WhatsApp number when you message us, place an order, or are registered as our customer or vendor.
  • Order and transaction information — parts enquired about or ordered, quantities, prices, invoices, credit notes, delivery details and payment status.
  • Message content — messages, images (e.g. photos of order lists) and documents you send to our WhatsApp business numbers, used solely to process your enquiries and orders.

2. How we use information

  • To answer product availability and price enquiries and to process, confirm and deliver orders.
  • To send order status updates, invoices, credit notes, payment reminders and account statements.
  • To coordinate procurement and delivery with our vendors and warehouse team.
  • To send occasional offers or stock updates, from which you can opt out at any time by replying “STOP”.

3. WhatsApp Business Platform

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. to communicate with customers and vendors. Messages exchanged with our business numbers are processed in accordance with WhatsApp’s own Business Terms and Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your WhatsApp data for purposes unrelated to our business relationship with you.

4. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to serve you: with our logistics/delivery partners for shipping, with our suppliers to fulfil your order, and with service providers (such as hosting and messaging platforms) who process data on our behalf. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

5. Data retention and security

We retain business records (orders, invoices, ledgers) as required for legitimate business and legal purposes, including under applicable Indian tax and company law. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised staff and systems, protected by authentication and access controls.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements) by contacting us at the details below. To stop receiving promotional messages, reply “STOP” on WhatsApp.

7. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, send a WhatsApp message or email to the contacts below with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. We will confirm completion within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.
